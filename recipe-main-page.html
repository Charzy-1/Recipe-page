Red Wine Recipe
Big Boyz Doinz

Bone-in short ribs, braised low and slow in red wine until the meat falls apart on its own — then shredded back into a sauce that's reduced until it clings to every ribbon of pappardelle.

By Charles Adiks · Updated August 2026 · ⭐ 4.8 (612 ratings)

30 minPrep Time
3½ hrsCook Time
4 hrsTotal Time
4–6Servings

This is the recipe I make when the weather turns and I want the whole house to smell like it for the rest of the day. Bone-in short ribs are seared hard for real crust, then braised in red wine with a classic soffritto until the meat needs nothing more than a fork to fall apart.

Don't rush the braise — the long, slow hours in the oven are doing all the work, breaking down connective tissue into silky gelatin that turns the sauce glossy on its own, no thickener required.

Ingredients

Makes 4-6 servings. Tap an item to check it off your list.

  • 3 lbs bone-in beef short ribs

Preparation

  1. 01

    Sear the short ribs

    Pat the short ribs dry and season generously with salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high until shimmering, then sear the ribs on all sides until deeply browned, about 8-10 minutes total. Work in batches if needed — a crowded pot steams instead of searing. Transfer to a plate.

    ≈ 12 min
  2. 02

    Build the soffritto

    Lower the heat to medium and add the onion, carrot, and celery to the fat left in the pot. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and just starting to turn golden at the edges, about 8 minutes.

    ≈ 12 min
  3. 03

    Bloom the tomato paste

    Stir in the garlic and tomato paste and cook until the paste darkens slightly and smells toasty rather than raw, about 90 seconds. This step matters — it builds real depth before any liquid goes in.

    ≈ 2 min
  4. 04

    Deglaze with wine

    Pour in the red wine, scraping up every browned bit from the bottom of the pot. Simmer until the wine has reduced by about half, roughly 6–7 minutes.

    ≈ 7 min
  5. 05

    Braise low and slow

    Add the beef stock, crushed tomatoes, bay leaves, thyme, and rosemary. Nestle the short ribs back into the pot so they're mostly submerged. Bring to a bare simmer, cover, and transfer to a 325°F oven for 3 to 3½ hours, until the meat shreds easily with a fork.

    3–3½ hrs
